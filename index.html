About the Author

Mijo Radalj

Mijo Radalj, born in Metković, spent many cherished days in Dubrovnik, where history, beauty, and an enduring spirit captured his imagination.

In gratitude to the city that inspired him so deeply, he created the Saga of Ragusa.

Through fiction, he seeks to bring the people, struggles, triumphs, and spirit of the medieval Adriatic back to life for modern readers.
